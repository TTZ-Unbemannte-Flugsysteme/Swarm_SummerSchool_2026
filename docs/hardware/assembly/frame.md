# Frame & Landing Gear Assembly

## Attaching Arms & Legs

![Mounting Overview](../../assets/mounting.png)

1. **Position the Bottom PDB**: Place the bottom Power Distribution Board (PDB) flat on your workspace. This forms the base of the central body.
2. **Sandwich the Arms**: Insert the root of each F450 arm between the bottom PDB and the top carbon/glass fiber plate. Carefully align the mounting holes.
3. **Attach the Landing Legs**: Position the plastic landing gear (legs) underneath the arms at the mounting points so the screw holes align completely through.
4. **Secure with Screws**: Drop the hex screws through the top plate, through the arms, and thread them firmly into the landing legs using a 2.0mm Hex Driver. Tighten in a cross-pattern to ensure even pressure.
