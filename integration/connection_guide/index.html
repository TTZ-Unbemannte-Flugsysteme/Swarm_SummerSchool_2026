    Motor pads run S11 S4 S3 S2 S1 top-to-bottom on the outer right column — so S1 is the lowest of the four, not the top. Easy to solder the quad backwards.

    2 · Power — where everything gets its volts

    The LiPo is the only power source. It feeds the PDB, which feeds the ESCs (raw voltage) and the FC's Vbat pad. The flight controller regulates that down itself and hands out 4V5, 5V and 9V to peripherals. The GPS and receiver take power from the FC, never from the battery.
    WhatPowered fromVoltageYou wire it?
    STM32 chipInternal 3.3V regulator, fed from Vbat3.3 V No — entirely internal
    The boardLiPo via PDB → Vbat + G2S–6S raw Yes — two wires
    GEP-M10 GPSFC 4V5 pad4.5 V Yes — from FC, never battery
    FrSky receiverFC 4V5 pad4.5 V Yes — from FC
    ESCs / motorsPDB directlyRaw battery Yes — power from PDB, signal to S1–S4
    Never put battery voltage on the GPS. It expects ~5V; raw LiPo at 11.1–14.8V destroys it instantly. If you are running a wire from the PDB to the GPS, stop.
    If your ESCs have BECs, connect at most one — ideally none. The FC has its own regulator, and two supplies fighting on one rail kills flight controllers.
    Why USB alone works on the bench. USB-C powers the STM32 and the peripheral rails, which is how parameters and firmware are read and written with no battery. But USB cannot spin motors — ESCs draw from the PDB. A motor test needs the main battery.

    3 · Receiver, compass and GPS — one block of pads

    All three live on the bottom edge, lower row. Ten pads, three devices.

    PadGoes toWireNote
    GFrSky RXGNDBlack
    4V5FrSky RXVCCRed — 4.5V, not 5V
    Rx2FrSky RXSBUS outThe signal wire
    Tx2FrSky RXF.PortOnly for F.Port; leave off for SBUS
    SCLCompassSCLI2C clock
    SDACompassSDAI2C data
    GGEP-M10GNDBlack
    4V5GEP-M10VCCRed
    Rx3GEP-M10TXCrossed
    Tx3GEP-M10RXCrossed
    Cross TX and RX. Rx3 takes the GPS's TX wire, Tx3 takes its RX. Straight-through is the most common GPS mistake — you get a lit LED but never a fix.
    No inversion setting needed — verified working. The receiver reports 16 live channels with SERIAL2_OPTIONS=0. Earlier guidance said SBUS needs 3; on this board and receiver it does not. Leave it at 0.
    Not Rx1/Tx1. Those are on the top edge (UART1 = telemetry). The GPS pads down here are Rx3/Tx3.
    Both SCL/SDA pairs are one bus in parallel — use whichever is easier to reach. A compass here is what finally clears COMPASS_DEV_ID=0.

    Props off for every motor test. ArduPilot spins one motor at a time with no stabilization — the airframe can lurch.
    Test letters are not motor numbers. Mission Planner steps clockwise from front-right, so B drives M4 and C drives M2. Don't read that as miswiring.
    ESC protocol is plain PWM (MOT_PWM_TYPE=0). Correct for standard PWM ESCs. For BLHeli_S set MOT_PWM_TYPE=6 (DShot600) — but only if the ESCs support it.
